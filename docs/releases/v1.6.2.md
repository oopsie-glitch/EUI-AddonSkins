# EUI AddonSkins 1.6.2

## Atlas and Auctionator Improvements

- Added an Atlas skin for Retail and Forever: cohesive map-window surfaces, selectors, search, navigation and settings while preserving native map artwork, labels, pins and meaningful colours.
- Atlas retains its round globe and adds accent-synchronized selector bars and full-field dropdown clicks. Reduced redundant styling during reopening, map updates and unrelated addon loads.
- Improved Auctionator item-quality borders and selling-item frames, fixed clipped empty previews, and removed exposed native inventory-slot backgrounds. Existing Retail and Forever support is retained; layouts vary with the installed Auctionator build.

## Requirements and installation

Requires EllesmereUI with Blizzard Skins+ enabled. Install supported addons separately for your client.

Download **EUIAddonSkins-1.6.2.zip**, extract its **EUIAddonSkins** folder into **Interface/AddOns/** and replace the previous folder. Keep SavedVariables to retain settings, then restart the game.

Includes 42 supported skins under the existing MIT license. Waypoint UI and World Quest Tracker remain excluded.

## Verification notes

The combined release passes compilation, regressions, manifests, license and packaging validation. Atlas appearance was accepted by the owner. Performance results are simulated adapter measurements; the reported in-game reopening stall has not been independently reproduced or proven fixed. Auctionator's new item-frame appearance was developed against Retail and retains guarded fallbacks for different client layouts.

The separate Auctionator Options-panel follow-up and proposed licensing changes are not part of this release.
